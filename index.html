    Our Process

    Concrete prep is everything.

    Concrete is porous. It soaks up oil, moisture and chemicals, which is why bare garage floors stain, crack and dust. Coatings that just sit on top peel. Ours bond into the slab.

    1. Diamond grindMechanical grinding opens the surface pores for maximum adhesion. No acid etching shortcuts.
    2. Repair & fillCracks repaired and control joints filled so the finished floor is seamless.
    3. Base coat & vapor barrierA specialized moisture-vapor barrier and pigmented base coat go down first.
    4. Flake broadcast or metallic pourFlake is broadcast across the whole surface, or metallic is hand-worked for movement.
    5. Polyaspartic topcoatUV-stable, won't yellow, resists oil and gas, and cures far faster than standard epoxy.
